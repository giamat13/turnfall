# Turnfall privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Turnfall does not collect, send, share or sell personal data.

- **No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.**
- **No network access.** The app has no permission to use the internet and does not connect to any server.
- **Progress and settings stay on your device.** Your best result on each level, your daily puzzle results, and your settings (speed, theme, sound and vibration) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Turnfall removes them. If your phone backs up apps, Android may include this data in that backup, as it does for other apps.
- **Vibration.** Turnfall vibrates the phone on some moves. You can turn this off in Settings. This is the only permission the app asks for (`VIBRATE`).
- **Saved pictures.** When you long-press a chapter map and save it, Turnfall writes a picture to `Pictures/Turnfall` in your gallery. Nothing is saved unless you choose to save it.

This policy applies to the Turnfall app for Android. If it changes, the new version is published in this file in the project repository.
